🧪 CHEMISTRY LESSON

Acids & Bases

Learn the properties of acids and bases, indicators, pH and neutralisation reactions.

🔬 What is an Acid?

An acid is a substance that produces hydrogen ions (H⁺) when dissolved in water.

Properties of Acids

  • They have a pH below 7.
  • They turn blue litmus paper red.
  • They react with some metals.
  • They react with bases.

Examples

  • Hydrochloric acid (HCl)
  • Sulfuric acid (H₂SO₄)
  • Nitric acid (HNO₃)
  • Ethanoic acid (CH₃COOH)

⚗️ What is a Base?

A base is a substance that reacts with an acid to form salt and water. Soluble bases are called alkalis.

Properties of Bases

  • They have a pH above 7.
  • They turn red litmus paper blue.
  • They react with acids.
  • Alkalis dissolve in water.

Examples

  • Sodium hydroxide (NaOH)
  • Potassium hydroxide (KOH)
  • Calcium hydroxide [Ca(OH)₂]
  • Magnesium oxide (MgO)

🌈 Indicators

An indicator is a substance that changes colour depending on whether a solution is acidic or alkaline.

Indicator Acid Alkali
Blue litmus Red Blue
Red litmus Red Blue
Phenolphthalein Colourless Pink

⚡ Neutralisation

Neutralisation occurs when an acid reacts with a base to form salt and water.

General Equation
Acid + Base → Salt + Water
