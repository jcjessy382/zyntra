⚡ PHYSICS • ENERGY

Master Energy

Understand energy, work, power and the different forms of energy.

⚡ What is Energy?

Energy is the ability to do work.

Energy can be transferred from one object or system to another and can change from one form to another.

🔋 Forms of Energy

  • Kinetic energy — energy possessed by a moving object.
  • Potential energy — stored energy due to position or condition.
  • Thermal energy — energy associated with temperature and heat.
  • Chemical energy — energy stored in chemical substances.
  • Electrical energy — energy associated with electric charges.
  • Light energy — energy carried by light.
  • Sound energy — energy transferred through vibrations.

🏃 Kinetic Energy

Kinetic energy is the energy possessed by a body because of its motion.

KE = ½mv²

m = mass in kilograms (kg)
v = velocity in metres per second (m/s)

📍 Gravitational Potential Energy

Gravitational potential energy is the energy stored in an object because of its position in a gravitational field.

GPE = mgh

m = mass (kg)
g = gravitational acceleration (m/s²)
h = height (m)

🛠️ Work Done

Work is done when a force causes an object to move through a distance in the direction of the force.

W = F × d

W = work done in joules (J)
F = force in newtons (N)
d = distance in metres (m)

🚀 Power

Power is the rate at which work is done or energy is transferred.

P = W ÷ t

P = power in watts (W)
W = work done in joules (J)
t = time in seconds (s)

🧠 Worked Example

A force of 20 N moves an object through a distance of 5 m. Calculate the work done.

W = F × d

W = 20 × 5

Work done = 100 J

🔄 Energy Transformation

Energy can change from one form to another.

  • Battery → electrical energy
  • Electric bulb → electrical energy → light + thermal energy
  • Car engine → chemical energy → kinetic energy + thermal energy
  • Hydroelectric power → gravitational potential energy → electrical energy
