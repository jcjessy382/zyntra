🧪 What is a Chemical Reaction?

A chemical reaction is a process in which one or more substances change to form new substances with different properties.

⚗️ Types of Chemical Reactions

1. Combination

Two or more substances combine to form one new substance.

A + B → AB

2. Decomposition

One compound breaks down into two or more simpler substances.

AB → A + B

3. Displacement

One element replaces another element in a compound.

A + BC → AC + B

4. Neutralisation

An acid reacts with a base to form salt and water.

Acid + Base → Salt + Water
