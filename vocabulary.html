Vocabulary Skills

Master the most important vocabulary skills.

🔄

Synonyms

Words that have the same or nearly the same meaning.

Happy → Joyful
⚖️

Antonyms

Words that have opposite meanings.

Hot → Cold
💬

Idioms

Expressions whose meaning is different from the literal meaning of the words.

"Break the ice" → Start a conversation
🔗

Phrasal Verbs

A verb combined with another word to create a new meaning.

Give up → Stop trying
🧩

Word Formation

Learn how words change into nouns, verbs, adjectives and adverbs.

Educate → Education → Educational
📖

Context

Understand a word by looking at how it is used in a sentence.

The child was fragile.

Word Formation

One root word can create several related words.

Beauty Noun
Beautiful Adjective
Beautifully Adverb
Beautify Verb

Useful Idioms

Learn common expressions used in English.

Once in a blue moon

Something that happens very rarely.

Piece of cake

Something that is very easy to do.

Hit the nail on the head

To describe something exactly or correctly.

⚡ Quick Vocabulary Revision

  • Synonym: Similar meaning
  • Antonym: Opposite meaning
  • Idiom: Special expression
  • Context: Meaning from surrounding words
  • Phrasal verb: Verb + another word
  • Word formation: Creating related words
