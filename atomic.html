Atomic Structure

Understand what atoms are made of and how protons, neutrons and electrons are arranged.

📚 What is an Atom?

An atom is the smallest particle of an element that can take part in a chemical reaction.

Atoms contain three main subatomic particles: protons, neutrons and electrons.

⚛️ The Three Main Particles

🔴 Proton

Has a positive charge and is found inside the nucleus.

⚪ Neutron

Has no charge and is found inside the nucleus.

🔵 Electron

Has a negative charge and moves around the nucleus.
