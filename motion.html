Understanding Motion

Learn how objects move and how we describe their movement.

📚 What is Motion?

Motion is the change in position of an object with time relative to a reference point.

For example, when a car moves from one place to another, its position changes with time.

📏 Distance

Distance is the total length of the path travelled by an object.

  • It is a scalar quantity.
  • Its SI unit is metre (m).
  • Distance has magnitude but no direction.

➡️ Displacement

Displacement is the distance moved by an object in a specified direction from its starting point.

  • It is a vector quantity.
  • It has both magnitude and direction.
  • Its SI unit is metre (m).

🏃 Speed

Speed is the distance travelled per unit time.

Speed = Distance ÷ Time

SI unit: metres per second (m/s)

🚗 Velocity

Velocity is the rate of change of displacement with time.

Velocity = Displacement ÷ Time

SI unit: metres per second (m/s)

📈 Acceleration

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity with time.

a = (v - u) ÷ t

Where:
u = initial velocity
v = final velocity
t = time
