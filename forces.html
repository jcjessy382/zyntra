Understanding Forces

Discover how pushes and pulls affect the motion of objects.

💪 What is Force?

A force is a push or a pull that can change the state of motion or shape of an object.

Force can make an object start moving, stop moving, change direction or change shape.

  • Force is a vector quantity.
  • The SI unit of force is the Newton (N).
  • Force has both magnitude and direction.

🧲 Types of Forces

  • Gravitational force — attraction between masses.
  • Frictional force — opposes motion between surfaces.
  • Magnetic force — attraction or repulsion involving magnets.
  • Electrostatic force — force between electric charges.
  • Upthrust — upward force exerted by a fluid.

🍎 Newton's Second Law

Newton's Second Law states that the acceleration of an object depends on the force applied and its mass.

F = ma

F = force in Newtons (N)
m = mass in kilograms (kg)
a = acceleration in metres per second squared (m/s²)

🧮 Worked Example

A force of 20 N acts on an object of mass 5 kg. Find its acceleration.

a = F ÷ m

a = 20 ÷ 5

Acceleration = 4 m/s²

🛑 Friction

Friction is a force that opposes relative motion between surfaces in contact.

Friction can be useful. For example, it helps us walk, stop vehicles and hold objects.
