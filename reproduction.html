Learn how organisms produce new individuals and how reproduction ensures the continuation of species.

🌱 What is Reproduction?

Reproduction is the biological process by which living organisms produce new individuals of the same species.

Reproduction is important because it ensures the continuity and survival of species.

🔬 Types of Reproduction

🌿 Asexual Reproduction

Asexual reproduction involves one parent and does not involve the fusion of male and female gametes.

  • Only one parent is involved.
  • Usually produces genetically similar offspring.
  • It can occur relatively quickly.

🧬 Sexual Reproduction

Sexual reproduction involves the fusion of male and female gametes to form a zygote.

  • Male and female gametes are involved.
  • Fertilisation occurs.
  • Offspring show genetic variation.

🌸 Reproduction in Plants

Flowering plants reproduce sexually through flowers. The flower contains reproductive structures that produce male and female gametes.

🌼 Male Part

The stamen is the male reproductive part of a flower. It consists mainly of the anther and filament.

🌺 Female Part

The carpel or pistil is the female reproductive part. It includes the stigma, style and ovary.

🐝 Pollination

Pollination is the transfer of pollen grains from the anther to the stigma of a flower.

Types of pollination:
  • Self-pollination — pollen is transferred to the stigma of the same flower or another flower on the same plant.
  • Cross-pollination — pollen is transferred from a flower on one plant to a flower on another plant of the same species.

🧬 Fertilisation

Fertilisation is the fusion of the male and female gametes to form a zygote.

The zygote develops into an embryo and eventually forms a new organism.

👨‍👩‍👧 Human Reproduction

In humans, reproduction involves male and female reproductive systems. The male produces sperm cells, while the female produces ova (egg cells).

♂ Male Reproductive System

  • Testes produce sperm cells.
  • Sperm are male gametes.
  • The reproductive system transports sperm.

♀ Female Reproductive System

  • Ovaries produce ova.
  • Ova are female gametes.
  • The reproductive system provides the environment for development after fertilisation.

🧠 Role of Hormones

Hormones help control reproductive processes and changes during puberty.

  • Testosterone is an important male reproductive hormone involved in development of male secondary sexual characteristics and sperm production.
  • Oestrogen is an important female reproductive hormone involved in development of female secondary sexual characteristics and the menstrual cycle.
  • Progesterone helps maintain the lining of the uterus during the menstrual cycle and pregnancy.
