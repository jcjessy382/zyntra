🧬 BIOLOGY • ECOLOGY

Understand Our Ecosystem.

Explore how organisms interact with one another and with their environment.

🌍 What is Ecology?

Ecology is the study of the relationships between living organisms and their environment.

An organism's environment includes both living and non-living factors.

🌱 Biotic and Abiotic Factors

🦁 Biotic Factors

Biotic factors are the living components of an ecosystem.

  • Plants
  • Animals
  • Microorganisms
  • Predators
  • Competitors

☀️ Abiotic Factors

Abiotic factors are the non-living components of an ecosystem.

  • Temperature
  • Light intensity
  • Water
  • Soil
  • Humidity

🏞️ Ecosystem

An ecosystem consists of living organisms interacting with one another and with the non-living components of their environment.

Examples include forests, lakes, grasslands and ponds.

🌿 Food Chains

A food chain shows how energy and food are transferred from one organism to another.

🌱 Grass → 🦗 Grasshopper → 🐸 Frog → 🐍 Snake → 🦅 Eagle

The arrows show the direction in which food and energy are transferred.

🕸️ Food Webs

A food web consists of several interconnected food chains within an ecosystem.

Food webs give a more complete picture of feeding relationships in an ecosystem.

🔋 Energy Flow

The main source of energy for most ecosystems is the Sun.

  • Plants capture light energy during photosynthesis.
  • Herbivores obtain energy by eating plants.
  • Carnivores obtain energy by eating other animals.
  • Decomposers break down dead organic matter.

🌱 Producers and Consumers

🌿 Producers

Producers make their own food, usually through photosynthesis. Green plants are common producers.

🦁 Consumers

Consumers obtain energy by feeding on other organisms. Animals are examples of consumers.

🍄 Decomposers

Decomposers such as fungi and bacteria break down dead organisms and waste materials.

They return nutrients to the environment, where the nutrients can be reused by plants.

⚔️ Competition

Competition occurs when organisms require the same limited resources.

Organisms may compete for resources such as food, water, light, space and mates.

📌 Quick Revision

  • Ecology studies relationships between organisms and their environment.
  • Biotic factors are living components.
  • Abiotic factors are non-living components.
  • An ecosystem contains living and non-living components interacting together.
  • Plants are important producers.
  • Animals are consumers.
  • Fungi and bacteria can act as decomposers.
  • Food chains show feeding relationships.
  • The Sun is the main energy source for most ecosystems.
  • Competition occurs when organisms require limited resources.
