🧬 What is a Cell?

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. All living organisms are made up of cells.

Some organisms consist of only one cell, while others are made up of many cells.

🔬 Main Cell Organelles

Cell membrane

Controls the movement of substances into and out of the cell.

Cytoplasm

The jelly-like substance where many chemical reactions take place.

Nucleus

Contains genetic material and controls many activities of the cell.

Mitochondria

The site of aerobic respiration and releases energy for cell activities.

Ribosomes

Sites where proteins are made.

Vacuole

Stores cell sap in plant cells and helps maintain the shape of the cell.

Chloroplast

Contains chlorophyll and is the site of photosynthesis in plant cells.

Cell wall

Provides support and protection to plant cells.

🧠 Why are Cells Important?

Cells carry out the processes necessary for life. These include respiration, growth, reproduction, excretion and nutrition.

In multicellular organisms, cells can become specialised to perform particular functions.

📝 Quick Revision

  • The nucleus controls many cell activities.
  • Mitochondria are involved in aerobic respiration.
  • Chloroplasts are involved in photosynthesis.
  • The cell membrane controls movement of substances.
  • The cell wall supports and protects plant cells.
  • Ribosomes are involved in protein synthesis.
