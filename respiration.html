Release energy.

Learn how living organisms release energy from food for use in their life processes.

🫁 What is Respiration?

Respiration is the process in which energy is released from food substances, especially glucose, for use by living cells.

The energy released is used for processes such as growth, movement, active transport and maintaining body temperature.

⚡ Aerobic Respiration

Aerobic respiration takes place in the presence of oxygen. It releases a large amount of energy.

Word equation:

Glucose + Oxygen → Carbon dioxide + Water + Energy

🔬 Anaerobic Respiration

Anaerobic respiration occurs when there is little or no oxygen available. It releases less energy than aerobic respiration.

🧑‍🦱 In Human Muscles

In muscles, anaerobic respiration produces lactic acid and releases a small amount of energy.

🍞 In Yeast

Yeast carries out anaerobic respiration and produces ethanol, carbon dioxide and energy.

🏭 Where Does Respiration Occur?

Most aerobic respiration occurs in the mitochondria of cells.

The mitochondria are sometimes called the powerhouses of the cell because they are major sites of energy release.

🌱 Respiration in Plants

Plants also respire. They use oxygen and release carbon dioxide during aerobic respiration.

Respiration occurs continuously in living plant cells, both during the day and at night.

🔄 Aerobic vs Anaerobic Respiration

Aerobic

  • Requires oxygen.
  • Releases a large amount of energy.
  • Produces carbon dioxide and water.

Anaerobic

  • Does not require oxygen.
  • Releases less energy.
  • Produces different products depending on the organism.

📌 Quick Revision

  • Respiration releases energy from food.
  • Aerobic respiration requires oxygen.
  • Aerobic respiration releases more energy.
  • Anaerobic respiration occurs without oxygen.
  • Most aerobic respiration occurs in mitochondria.
  • Yeast produces ethanol and carbon dioxide during anaerobic respiration.
  • Muscles can produce lactic acid during anaerobic respiration.
