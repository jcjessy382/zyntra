🔤 Parts of Speech

Words perform different functions in sentences.

👤

Noun

A word used to name a person, place, thing or idea.

Example: teacher, Nairobi, book
🔄

Pronoun

A word used instead of a noun.

Example: he, she, they, it
⚡

Verb

A word that shows an action, occurrence or state.

Example: run, write, is
🎨

Adjective

A word that describes or modifies a noun or pronoun.

Example: beautiful, tall, clever
💨

Adverb

A word that modifies a verb, adjective or another adverb.

Example: quickly, very, carefully
🔗

Preposition

A word that shows the relationship between words.

Example: in, on, under, beside
🔀

Conjunction

A word used to connect words, phrases or clauses.

Example: and, but, because
❗

Interjection

A word or expression that shows sudden emotion.

Example: Wow! Oh! Hurray!
🧩

Determiner

A word that comes before a noun to clarify its reference.

Example: the, a, an, some

⏰ English Tenses

Tenses help show when an action happens.

Tense Example Use
Simple Present She plays football. Regular actions or facts.
Simple Past She played football. Completed past action.
Simple Future She will play football. Future action.
Present Continuous She is playing football. Action happening now.
Past Continuous She was playing football. Action in progress in the past.
Present Perfect She has played football. Past action connected to the present.

🎯 Subject-Verb Agreement

The subject and verb should agree in number.

Rule 1 — Singular subject

A singular subject normally takes a singular verb.

She plays football.
Rule 2 — Plural subject

A plural subject normally takes a plural verb.

They play football.
Rule 3 — Don't be confused by extra words

The words between the subject and verb do not necessarily change the agreement.

The boy, together with his friends, is here.

🔄 Active and Passive Voice

Learn how sentences can change their focus.

➡️

Active Voice

The subject performs the action.

The student answered the question.
🔄

Passive Voice

The subject receives the action.

The question was answered by the student.

🧩 Sentence Structure

Understand how words combine to form sentences.

1️⃣

Simple Sentence

Contains one main clause.

The student reads.
2️⃣

Compound Sentence

Contains two or more independent clauses joined together.

I studied, and I passed.
3️⃣

Complex Sentence

Contains a main clause and at least one dependent clause.

I studied because I wanted to pass.
