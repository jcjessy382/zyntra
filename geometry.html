What is Geometry?

Understanding shapes, sizes, positions and measurements.

📐 Geometry

Geometry is the branch of mathematics that deals with shapes, sizes, angles, positions and properties of objects. It helps us understand the space around us.


Geometry is used in construction, engineering, architecture, design, surveying and many other fields.

Important Formulas

Formulas you should remember.

Rectangle Area

A = l × w

Rectangle Perimeter

P = 2(l + w)

Triangle Area

A = ½ × b × h

Circle Area

A = πr²

Circumference

C = 2πr

Pythagoras

a² + b² = c²

Worked Example

Let's solve a geometry problem step by step.

🔺 Find the area of a triangle

Base = 10 cm

Height = 6 cm

Formula: A = ½ × b × h

A = ½ × 10 × 6

A = 5 × 6

✅ Answer: Area = 30 cm²

Pythagoras Example

Solve a right-angled triangle.

📐 Find the hypotenuse

Side a = 3 cm

Side b = 4 cm

Formula: a² + b² = c²

3² + 4² = c²

9 + 16 = c²

25 = c²

c = √25

✅ Answer: c = 5 cm

⚡ Quick Revision

Remember these key points.

📐 A right angle is 90°.
🔺 The angles in a triangle add up to 180°.
⭕ Diameter = 2 × radius.
📏 Perimeter is the distance around a shape.
📐 Area is the amount of surface covered by a shape.
📦 Volume is the amount of space occupied by a solid.
🔺 Pythagoras applies to right-angled triangles.
