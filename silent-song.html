FEATURED STORY

🌿 Ninema

Ninema is a story that provides an opportunity to examine social expectations, relationships, gender roles and the challenges faced by women.

For examination purposes, focus on how the characters' experiences reveal wider issues in society and how the writer develops these ideas through characterisation and other literary techniques.

✍️ Literary Techniques

Don't just identify a technique — explain its effect and purpose.

🖼️

Imagery

Language that creates a strong picture, feeling or sensory impression in the reader's mind.

💬

Dialogue

Conversation can reveal personality, relationships, conflict and attitudes.

👤

Characterisation

The writer's methods of presenting a character's personality and development.

🔁

Repetition

Repeated words or ideas can emphasise important feelings, themes or situations.

⚔️

Conflict

Conflict creates tension and helps develop characters, themes and the plot.

🎭

Irony

A contrast between expectation and reality that can highlight an important idea.

🧠 How to Analyse a Story

Use this four-step method when answering literature questions.

1

Identify

Identify the character, theme, technique or issue.

2

Evidence

Refer accurately to an event, action or relevant detail.

3

Explain

Explain what the evidence reveals and why it matters.

4

Link

Connect your explanation back to the question.

📝 KCSE-Style Practice

Practise developing complete literature answers.

THEME

Discuss the challenges faced by women in Ninema.

In your answer, identify relevant challenges, support each point with evidence from the story, and explain what each example reveals.

CHARACTER

Discuss the character traits revealed through the actions and experiences of the main character.

Give several well-developed points rather than simply listing character traits.

TECHNIQUE

How does the writer use literary techniques to communicate important ideas?

Identify techniques, give relevant references and explain their effect.

SOCIETY

What does the story reveal about society?

Connect events and character experiences to wider social issues.

⚡ Quick Revision

Remember these points before an examination.

🎯

Answer the Question

Always keep your points directly connected to the question.

📌

Use Evidence

Support your argument with accurate references to the story.

💡

Explain

Don't stop after giving evidence. Explain its significance.

🔗

Link

Connect your explanation back to the theme or question.

✍️

Organise

Use clear paragraphs and logical progression of ideas.

🔍

Analyse

Show what the writer's choices reveal, not just what happened.
