Explore the World of Light

Learn about reflection, refraction, mirrors, lenses and the properties of light.

💡 What is Light?

Light is a form of energy that enables us to see objects. It is electromagnetic radiation and can travel through a vacuum.

In a vacuum, light travels at approximately 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s.

🔦 Sources of Light

Objects that produce their own light are called luminous objects.

  • Natural source: the Sun.
  • Artificial sources: electric bulbs, candles and lamps.

🪞 Reflection of Light

Reflection is the bouncing back of light when it strikes a surface.

The law of reflection states that the angle of incidence is equal to the angle of reflection.

i = r

i = angle of incidence
r = angle of reflection

🔍 Refraction of Light

Refraction is the change in direction of light when it passes from one transparent medium to another because its speed changes.

For example, a pencil partly immersed in water may appear bent because of refraction.

🪞 Plane Mirrors

A plane mirror has a flat reflecting surface.

  • The image is upright.
  • The image is virtual.
  • The image is the same size as the object.
  • The image is laterally inverted.
  • The image is the same distance behind the mirror as the object is in front.

🔎 Lenses

A lens is a transparent material with curved surfaces that refracts light.

  • Convex lens — thicker at the centre and can converge parallel rays of light.
  • Concave lens — thinner at the centre and diverges parallel rays of light.

🌈 Dispersion of Light

Dispersion is the splitting of white light into its different colours.

A prism can separate white light into the colours of the visible spectrum.
