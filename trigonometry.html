📚 The Three Main Ratios

Remember the famous SOH-CAH-TOA rule.

sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse
SOH = Sine, Opposite, Hypotenuse

Trigonometry Formula Sheet

cos θ = Adjacent / Hypotenuse
tan θ = Opposite / Adjacent

🧠 Worked Example

Let's solve a simple trigonometry problem.

Example: Find the value of sin θ

Opposite = 6 cm
Hypotenuse = 10 cm
sin θ = Opposite ÷ Hypotenuse
sin θ = 6 ÷ 10
sin θ = 0.6

⚡ Quick Revision

Opposite ÷ Hypotenuse → Sine

Adjacent ÷ Hypotenuse → Cosine

Opposite ÷ Adjacent → Tangent
