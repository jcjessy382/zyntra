🧠 How to Answer Literature Questions

Use a clear structure when developing your answers.

1. Understand the question Identify exactly what the examiner wants you to discuss.
2. Make your point Begin with a clear statement that answers the question.
3. Give evidence Refer to a relevant character, event, action or detail from the text.
4. Explain Explain how your evidence supports your point.
5. Link Connect your explanation back to the question.
6. Develop Add another relevant point when the question requires a longer answer.

⭐ KCSE Literature Tips

Build strong examination habits.

Remember these points

✓ Know the important characters and their roles.

✓ Understand the major themes and how they are developed.

✓ Know important events in each story.

✓ Learn the literary techniques used by the writer.

✓ Support your answers with relevant evidence.

✓ Explain your points instead of simply listing them.

✓ Read the question carefully before answering.
