🧬 BIOLOGY • NUTRITION

Understand Nutrition.

Learn how living organisms obtain and use nutrients for energy, growth, repair and healthy functioning.

🍎 What is Nutrition?

Nutrition is the process by which living organisms obtain and use nutrients needed for energy, growth, repair and maintenance of the body.

🌱 Modes of Nutrition

There are two main modes of nutrition:

  • Autotrophic nutrition — organisms make their own food. Green plants make food through photosynthesis.
  • Heterotrophic nutrition — organisms obtain food from other organisms.

🥗 Main Nutrients

🍞 Carbohydrates

Provide energy for activities and body processes. Examples include rice, bread, maize and potatoes.

🥩 Proteins

Used for growth and repair of body tissues. Examples include beans, eggs, fish and meat.

🥑 Fats

Provide energy and help with insulation and protection of body organs.

🍊 Vitamins

Needed in small amounts to keep the body healthy and support normal body functions.

💧 Water

Important for chemical reactions, transport of substances and temperature regulation.

🧂 Mineral Salts

Needed for healthy growth and proper functioning of the body.

🍽️ Balanced Diet

A balanced diet contains all the essential nutrients in the correct proportions to meet the body's needs.

A healthy diet should contain appropriate amounts of carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, mineral salts, water and fibre.

📚 Examples of Nutrients and Sources

Nutrient Examples of Sources
Carbohydrates Maize, rice, bread, potatoes
Proteins Beans, eggs, fish, meat
Fats Oils, nuts, seeds
Vitamins Fruits and vegetables
Mineral salts Milk, vegetables, fruits
Water Drinking water, fruits and vegetables

⚡ Importance of Nutrition

  • Provides energy.
  • Promotes growth.
  • Repairs damaged tissues.
  • Helps maintain healthy body functions.
  • Supports the immune system.
  • Helps regulate body processes.
