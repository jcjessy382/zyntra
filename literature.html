🎭

Literature written mainly for performance. It uses characters, dialogue, scenes and stage directions.

📝

Poetry

Literature that uses carefully selected language, rhythm, imagery and other poetic techniques.

🧠 Elements of Literature

These are the building blocks you should identify when analysing a literary work.

👤 Characters

The people, animals or figures involved in the story. Study their qualities, actions, relationships and development.

🌍 Setting

The time, place and social environment where events happen.

📈 Plot

The sequence of events through which the story develops.

⚔️ Conflict

The struggle between opposing forces, characters, ideas or circumstances.

💡 Themes

Major ideas explored by the writer, such as education, equality, poverty or leadership.

🎨 Style

The way the writer uses language and literary techniques to communicate ideas.

🎨 Literary Techniques

Don't just identify a technique — explain its effect.

🔄

Repetition

Repeating words or ideas to emphasise an important point.

🪞

Imagery

Language that creates pictures or sensory experiences in the reader's mind.

💬

Dialogue

Words spoken by characters that can reveal personality, relationships and conflict.

⚡

Irony

A contrast between what is expected and what actually happens or is intended.

🔍

Symbolism

Using an object, action or image to represent a deeper idea.

🎭

Characterisation

The methods a writer uses to present and develop characters.

⭐ The ZYNTRA Analysis Method

Use this structure when answering Literature questions.

1

Identify

Identify the character, theme, technique or idea.

2

Evidence

Give a relevant event, action or detail from the text.

3

Explain

Explain what the evidence shows and why it matters.

4

Link

Connect your explanation back to the question.

⚡ Quick Revision

Ask yourself these questions when analysing a story.

Character Who?
Setting Where & when?
Plot What happens?
Theme What idea?
Conflict What struggle?
Style How?
