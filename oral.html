Pronunciation

Clear pronunciation makes spoken English easier to understand.

Important Areas

Vowels Sounds produced with relatively open airflow.
Consonants Sounds produced with some restriction of airflow.
Word endings Pronounce final sounds clearly.

🔊 Word Stress

Word stress means giving extra emphasis to one syllable in a word. Correct stress can make spoken English clearer.

PREsent

Stress can change depending on how a word is used.

Intonation

Intonation refers to the rise and fall of the voice when speaking.

↗ Rising Intonation

A rising pitch is commonly associated with many yes/no questions.


Example: Are you ready?

↘ Falling Intonation

A falling pitch is commonly used at the end of many statements.


Example: I am ready.

⚡ Improve Your Speaking

  • Speak slowly enough for listeners to understand.
  • Pronounce important words clearly.
  • Use appropriate pauses between ideas.
  • Listen carefully before responding.
  • Practise speaking English regularly.
  • Organise your ideas before giving a speech.
  • Maintain confidence even when you make a mistake.
