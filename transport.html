🧬 BIOLOGY • TRANSPORT

How substances move.

Learn how plants and animals transport water, minerals, gases, nutrients and other substances around their bodies.

🚚 What is Transport?

Transport is the movement of substances from one part of an organism to another.

Transport is important because cells need substances such as water, oxygen and nutrients, while waste products need to be removed.

🌱 Transport in Plants

Plants have specialised tissues that transport substances throughout the plant.

💧 Xylem

Xylem transports water and mineral salts from the roots to other parts of the plant.

🍃 Phloem

Phloem transports manufactured food, especially sugars, from leaves to other parts of the plant.

💧 Movement of Water in Plants

  • Water enters the roots mainly through root hairs.
  • Water moves through the xylem.
  • Water is transported upwards to the leaves.
  • Water is eventually lost from leaves through transpiration.

🩸 Transport in Humans

Humans have a transport system made mainly of the heart, blood and blood vessels.

❤️ Heart

The heart pumps blood around the body.

🩸 Blood

Blood transports oxygen, nutrients, hormones, carbon dioxide and other substances.

🔴 Arteries

Arteries carry blood away from the heart.

🔵 Veins

Veins carry blood towards the heart.

🔬 Blood Components

  • Red blood cells — transport oxygen.
  • White blood cells — help defend the body against disease.
  • Platelets — help blood clot.
  • Plasma — transports dissolved substances such as nutrients and carbon dioxide.

🫀 Double Circulation

Humans have a double circulatory system. Blood passes through the heart twice during one complete circuit of the body.

  • Pulmonary circulation carries blood between the heart and lungs.
  • Systemic circulation carries blood between the heart and the rest of the body.

📌 Quick Revision

  • Xylem → water and mineral salts.
  • Phloem → manufactured food.
  • Heart → pumps blood.
  • Arteries → away from the heart.
  • Veins → towards the heart.
  • Red blood cells → transport oxygen.
  • White blood cells → defence.
  • Platelets → blood clotting.
  • Plasma → transports dissolved substances.
