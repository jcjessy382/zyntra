What is Statistics?

Turning data into useful information.

📊 Statistics

Statistics is the branch of mathematics concerned with collecting, organizing, presenting, analyzing and interpreting data.


Statistics is useful in science, business, medicine, education, sports, economics and everyday decision-making.

Important Formulas

Formulas to remember.

Mean

Mean = Σx ÷ n

Probability

P(E) = Favourable outcomes ÷ Total outcomes

Range

Range = Highest − Lowest

Percentage

Percentage = Part ÷ Whole × 100

Worked Example

Find the mean, median and mode.

🧮 Data Set

2, 4, 4, 6, 9


Step 1 — Mean

Mean = (2 + 4 + 4 + 6 + 9) ÷ 5

Mean = 25 ÷ 5

Mean = 5

Step 2 — Median

The middle value is 4.

Median = 4

Step 3 — Mode

The value appearing most often is 4.

Mode = 4

⚡ Quick Revision

Remember these key ideas.

📊 Data is information collected for analysis.
➗ Mean is calculated by dividing the total by the number of values.
🎯 Median is the middle value when data is arranged in order.
🔢 Mode is the most frequently occurring value.
📏 Range is the highest value minus the lowest value.
🎲 Probability measures how likely an event is to occur.
