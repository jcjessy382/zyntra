Passage 1 — The Power of Reading

Read the passage carefully before answering the questions.

The Power of Reading

Reading is one of the most useful habits a student can develop. It exposes learners to new ideas, information and different ways of thinking. A student who reads regularly is likely to encounter words that may not appear in everyday conversations.

Reading also improves a learner's ability to understand information. When students read stories, newspapers, textbooks and other materials, they learn how writers organise ideas. They also learn how evidence can be used to support an argument.

However, reading should not be limited to examination preparation. Reading for pleasure can make learning more enjoyable. A student who develops a regular reading habit can gradually become more confident when speaking and writing.

Therefore, students should make reading part of their daily routine. Even a short period of focused reading can contribute to the development of knowledge and communication skills.

1. What is the main idea of the passage?

Give the central message presented by the writer.

Reading is a valuable habit that improves knowledge, understanding and communication skills.
2. Mention two benefits of reading given in the passage.

Identify any two benefits mentioned by the writer.

Reading exposes students to new ideas and improves their understanding of information. It can also improve speaking and writing skills.
3. Why should reading not be limited to examination preparation?

Explain the reason given by the writer.

Reading for pleasure can make learning more enjoyable and can help students develop a regular reading habit.
4. What does the word "gradually" mean in the passage?

Use the context to determine its meaning.

Gradually means slowly or over a period of time.

Passage 2 — Technology and Learning

Try this passage after completing Passage 1.

Technology and Learning

Technology has changed the way students access information. In the past, learners depended heavily on printed books and classroom notes. Today, students can use computers and other digital devices to find educational information quickly.

Digital learning can make education more flexible. A learner can watch an educational lesson, practise questions or read digital material at a convenient time. Technology can also make it easier for students to communicate and share ideas.

However, technology must be used responsibly. Having access to a large amount of information does not automatically mean that all the information is accurate. Students should therefore learn to evaluate information before accepting it as true.

The best approach is to combine technology with good study habits. Digital tools can support learning, but students still need concentration, discipline and critical thinking.

1. How has technology changed access to information?

Give one change described in the passage.

Students can use computers and digital devices to find educational information quickly.
2. Why should students evaluate information?

Give the reason provided by the writer.

Not all information available through technology is necessarily accurate.
3. Name two qualities students still need when using digital learning tools.

Identify two qualities from the final paragraph.

Concentration, discipline and critical thinking are needed. Any two are acceptable.

⚡ Comprehension Exam Tips

  • Read the entire passage before answering.
  • Underline important words and ideas.
  • Answer using information from the passage.
  • For word meanings, study the surrounding sentence.
  • For inference questions, combine clues from the passage.
  • Keep your answers clear and relevant.
